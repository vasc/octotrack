Holds up lists the later steps of the sequence it forks from, and Nothing; picking one takes effect at once. Remove waits with Undo, like a line's moves; its issues go back onto step 2, the step it forked from. Rename or change its steps opens a sheet with its title and its steps, one per line. Owners do all of this from the CLI.

B · Make a branch its own line: the developer places it, up next or started; an agent's becomes a proposal

Make 3a its own line

Its steps and 2 issues move to the new line. Here it stays as a row that opens it, still holding up step 4 until that line is done.

The title comes from the branch and takes the focus; done when is asked for, as for every line. Both choices wait with Undo, like a line's moves. An owner runs octo plan promote L-2 3a --done-when "…": that proposes the line, recommended for up next, and it lands with the other proposals, still holding up step 4.

C · Edge cases

  • Done on a step that still waits. Picking Done on step 3 while 2a is open:Not changed: Step 3 waits on 2a, Order the USB-C breakout. Finish what it waits on, or set that branch to hold nothing up.
  • The branch's line is tabled. Step 4 reads: 4 Hang it in the hallway · waits on L-12, tabledso the stall shows on the plan. Dropped, or turned down as not a line, L-12 ends 3a: step 4 no longer waits on it, and its work went with that line.
  • The branch's line is done. 3a is ticked, and step 4 no longer waits.
  • No branches. The plan looks exactly as it does today; the aside drops "branches open".
